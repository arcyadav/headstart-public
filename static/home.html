HeadStart

A study planner for your semester. Give it your classes, exams, tasks and free time, and it plans when to study each thing so deadlines are met, and re-plans as you go.

Start your own planner

A blank planner just for you, at its own private link: set up your semester and try everything. Bookmark the link to come back to it.

Run it on your computer

HeadStart is open source: download it from GitHub and keep your planner on your own machine.

Get it on GitHub

What it does

  • Plans study sessions into the free time you paint on a weekly grid, within your daily study limits.
  • Adds a lecture to study after each class, and revision before each exam.
  • Finishes work before its deadline, in sensible session lengths with breaks, and tells you when something can't fit.
  • Re-plans whenever you log a session, mark a task done or a class is cancelled, keeping the rest of the plan stable.
