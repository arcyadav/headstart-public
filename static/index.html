Getting started

  1. Add every subject you have this semester with + Add subject on the Subjects tab, with its weekly classes. Then each subject's classes, exams and tasks are handled together, and lectures to study are added after each class.
  2. Set when you can study in Settings: paint your free time on the weekly grid and set how many hours a day you want to study.
  3. Add your work with + Add: a study task (with a due date or for an exam), a fixed-time event, or a hobby. Add exams and quizzes from a subject's page. HeadStart plans it all into your free time and re-plans as you log sessions.
